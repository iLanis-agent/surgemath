The joule truth

A joule rating is a budget that spends itself. Thirty absorbed spikes of fifty joules each leave a 2,000-joule strip with 500.0 joules honestly.

The clamp truth

Clamping voltage is where the protector starts fighting, not where it wins. A 330-volt clamp on a 120-volt line lets 210.0 volts past first.

The load truth

A 15-amp strip is rated 1,800 watts, but the honest continuous figure is 80% of that: 1,440.0 watts. A heater and a PC can run 260.0 watts over honestly.
