Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Joule ratings and let-through figures here are nameplate claims printed on the box, not measurements of your protector, your wiring or your devices. Follow the maker's documentation over anything this page says, and ask an electrician about your wiring.

The rating truth

A joule rating is how much energy the maker says the protector can absorb before it fails. Higher means greater rated capacity.

The clamp truth

The UL 1449 let-through rating is the voltage that can pass through after suppression. Lower means stronger protection.
